An Introduction to IGCSE

Cambridge IGCSE Literature in English (0475) offers students the opportunity to read, interpret, evaluate and respond to a range of literature in English. This range includes drama, prose and poetry from the works of Shakespeare to contemporary literature. This course allows students to deepen their understanding and appreciation of the ways in which writers use English to express meaning and achieve a range of effects and enables them to present an informed, personal response to the material they have studied. This syllabus will stimulate learners to read for pleasure and to explore wider and universal issues, promoting a better understanding of themselves and the world.

Cambridge IGCSE Combined Science (0653) gives students the opportunity to study Biology, Chemistry and Physics, each covered in separate syllabus sections. It is a single award qualification, earning one grade. The syllabus helps students to understand the biological and technological world in which they live, and take an informed interest in science and scientific developments. The syllabus also includes the basic principles and concepts that are fundamental to science, some current applications of biology, chemistry and physics, and a strong emphasis on practical skills.

Photo by Giu Vicente on Unsplash

Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics (0580) supports students in building competency, confidence and fluency in their use of techniques and mathematical understanding. Students develop a feel for quantity, patterns and relationships, as well as developing reasoning, problem-solving and analytical skills in a variety of abstract and real-life contexts. This syllabus provides a strong foundation of mathematical knowledge both for students studying mathematics at a higher level and those who will require mathematics to support skills in other subjects.
