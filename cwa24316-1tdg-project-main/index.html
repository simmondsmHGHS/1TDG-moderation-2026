IGCSE

International General Certificate of Secondary Education

Cambridge IGCSE is an extention programme designed to boarden students knowledge bases and further deepen critical thinking to achive academic success. At HGHS, we offer Cambridge IGCSE courses of English, Science and Mathematics. The entry to these courses is by invite and is opt-in. The English and Science courses runs over two years, starting from year 10 and ends with an examination at the end of year 11. The Mathematics course runs over one year in Year 11 with an examination at the end of the same year.
