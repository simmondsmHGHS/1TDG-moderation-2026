Fashion Club

Fashion club is a inviting place for everyone. It is a great place to be for Hamilton Girls High students, giving them a oppurtunity to showcase their designs and ideas in our annual fashion show.

Photo by NuKi Chikhladze on Unsplash
  • Explore your style: Experiment with unique looks in a highly supportive environment.
  • Learn hands-on skills: Gain practical experience in sewing, garment upcycling, and styling.
  • Build your resume: Develop leadership skills by planning large campus fashion shows.
  • Make like-minded friends: Connect instantly with people who share your creative passions.
  • Practice sustainability: Participate in eco-friendly campus events like community clothing swaps.
  • Boost your confidence: Gain self-assurance through creative expression and constructive peer feedback.
  • Network for careers: Meet industry professionals working in design, merchandising, and marketing.
