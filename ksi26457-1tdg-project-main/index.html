Science Club

Welcome to my Science Club, a place for curious minds who love exploring how the world works. In this club, you'll learn biology, discovering how humans, plants, and other living things grow, function, and interact. We will also explore physics, focusing on convection, conduction, and radiation. We'll learn key equations such as kinetic energy and gravitational potential energy, along with concepts like electrical energy, currents, and temperature. There's plenty more we can explore as the lessons grow. In chemistry, you'll get hands-on with exciting experiments while learning how chemical reactions happen and why they matter.Overall, this club is perfect for anyone who enjoys learning about science, from the tiny particles around us to the mysteries far beyond our galaxy. It's also a great place to make friends, have fun, and learn in a supportive environment where your curiosity leads the way.
