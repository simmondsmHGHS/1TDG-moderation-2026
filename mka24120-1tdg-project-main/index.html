The Bhangra Club is all about the rich history of Bhangra and all about learning the traditional folk dance. Bhangra is a very fast paced, up beat and high energy dance and there are many different variations and types of bhangra such as Jhumar, luddi, dhamaak, daankara. There are also modern variations such as fusion and free form where you can dance to pop songs with traditional dhol beats. Bhangra also is considered a very good workout if you're looking to get into shape. The Bhangra Club also offers Giddha classes where you can learn traditional circle Giddha, competitive and social Giddha. Traditional circle Giddha includes folk songs which you learn as you need to perform it. You learn different footwork variations such as single addi and double addi.
