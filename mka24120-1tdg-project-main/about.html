About

Here at The Bhangra Club, we focus on learning about the culture of Punjab and the history behind the folk songs used in bhangra or Giddha. We aim to teach our members about the significance of the folk dances and encourage them to take part in our group bonding activities where we do colouring or learn small modern variations of dance and put together small performances. The Bhangra Club actively takes part in upcoming and available competitions or shows where they can perform Bhangra or Giddha.
