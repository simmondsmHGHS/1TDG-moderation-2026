Community

In The Bhangra club community we have a group of girls so far who previously competed by the name of Balle Baddies last year and here are some images of some of our girls performing or competing in shows.

The Bhangra club community aims to create a safe space for the Panjabi community or anyone looking for a place to stand here in New Zealand and be part of an energetic dance club. If you'd like to join for the vibes, that's great too!
