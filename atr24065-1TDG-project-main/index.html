We have Meetings every Tuesday prior to Debate to disscuse the motion, teams and avalibilites. It is not complulsary to come to every meeting but we recommended coming to the meeting on the Thursday, which is the day of your debate! Our Student prefects will guide you along if there is any doubt about anything.
Practices are held in H15 and H14. There is training to learn and improve your skills. Fee for Debating competitions can range from $20 to $40 per person depending on the competition entered.
