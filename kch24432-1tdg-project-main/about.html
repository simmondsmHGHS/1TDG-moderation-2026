Information:

Joining our club costs $20 each year, and you must pay this fee before the new term begins. This money is very important because the club uses it to buy all the supplies, tools, and resources needed for our upcoming projects. By paying early, you help ensure that every member has exactly what they need to have a fun and successful year ahead!.
